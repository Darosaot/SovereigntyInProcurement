How competition impact is classified

Hard eligibility exclusion
Failure of the stated establishment, control, governance or eligible-country condition prevents participation.
Hard gate with limited exception
The main rule is restrictive, but a defined lot, supplier role or subcontractor may use a narrow exception or waiver.
Conditional participation / guarantee
Participation can remain possible after an ownership-control assessment, approved guarantee or mitigation measure.
Operational requirement only
The clause constrains hosting, data, personnel or delivery arrangements without excluding the bidder on ownership/control grounds.
